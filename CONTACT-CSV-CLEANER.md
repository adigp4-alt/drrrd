# Messy contact export? See exactly what stays, what changes, and what needs review.

Duplicate rows and conflicting contact details make a CSV hard to trust. Contact CSV Cleaner gives each record a clear destination: accepted, held for review, or confirmed as an exact duplicate. Your original stays preserved.
