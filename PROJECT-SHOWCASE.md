## What to inspect

- [Forecast routes](./app/routes/forecast.py): forecast-board, scorecard, diagnostics, historical replay, and resolution endpoints.
- [Forecast ledger](./app/forecast_ledger.py): probability scoring and calibration calculations.
- [Reporting/export routes](./app/routes/export.py): report and Excel export implementation.

## Explore or discuss the work

Inspect the linked source and documented project scope. For cleanup inquiries, [ask about fit — inquiry only](https://github.com/adigp4-alt/drrrd/issues/32) and comment with your approximate row count, column headers, and desired output. GitHub sign-in is required and comments are public. Keep personal records out of comments and agree a private delivery channel before sharing a source CSV.

**A comment does not place an order or collect payment.** The proposed service covers one compatible CSV, up to 2,000 records and 15 columns, agreed cleanup rules, exact-duplicate handling, a review queue, an audit, and one revision within scope. Target delivery is 24 hours after a usable file, rules, and payment are confirmed. Six fields must be mapped: name, email, phone, company, city, and country. Email-deliverability verification, enrichment, scraping, and uncertain-match merging are excluded.

The Flask app's `/contact-cleanup` page keeps its payment button disabled unless an operator configures a valid hosted payment URL and explicitly enables checkout after verification. The [handoff guide](./docs/contact-cleanup-checkout.md) records configuration and the remaining portfolio integration. **No live checkout or completed payment is claimed.** A paid order requires the provider's payment confirmation and seller confirmation of the agreed scope; opening a link or returning to the page is not proof of payment.
